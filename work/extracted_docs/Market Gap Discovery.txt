[Heading 1] Market Gap Discovery (MGD)
[Heading 2] Version
[First Paragraph] 0.1
[Heading 2] Purpose
[First Paragraph] The purpose of this document is to identify and validate the market gaps that existing AI products fail to address.
[Body Text] Rather than focusing on technology, this document analyzes business operations, customer workflows, competitor limitations, and unmet business capabilities.
[Body Text] The outcome of this document is to determine why our company should exist and where we can create measurable business value.
[Heading 1] Executive Summary
[First Paragraph] The current AI market has matured rapidly.
[Body Text] Businesses now have access to hundreds of AI chatbots, RAG platforms, AI copilots, and agent frameworks.
[Body Text] However, despite rapid adoption, most organizations continue to struggle with operational inefficiencies because existing AI products primarily focus on conversation rather than business execution.
[Body Text] Our research indicates that businesses are not looking for another chatbot.
[Body Text] They are looking for an AI platform that understands their business, integrates with their existing systems, and performs meaningful work.
[Body Text] This document identifies those gaps.

[First Paragraph] Most platforms ask customers to build AI.
[Body Text] Our platform should ask customers to describe their business.
[Body Text] Instead of:
[Compact] Build an Agent
