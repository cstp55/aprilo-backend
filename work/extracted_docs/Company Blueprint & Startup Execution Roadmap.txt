[Heading 1] Founder Commitment
[First Paragraph] Every feature must satisfy three conditions before implementation:
[Compact] Supported by evidence.
[Compact] Creates measurable customer value.
[Compact] Strengthens the company’s long-term competitive advantage.
[First Paragraph] If a feature fails any of these conditions, it is deferred regardless of implementation effort.
[Body Text] This principle ensures that the company remains focused on solving meaningful business problems rather than accumulating features.
