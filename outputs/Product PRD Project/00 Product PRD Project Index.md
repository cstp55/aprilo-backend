## MVP Definition

The MVP is not a generic HR chatbot. It is an operational AI assistant for HR knowledge and workflow support.

The first product should help employees and HR teams:

- Answer HR policy and company knowledge questions with source citations.
- Reduce repetitive HR questions.
- Support onboarding and employee self-service.
- Route unresolved or sensitive questions to a human HR owner.
- Respect roles, permissions, and knowledge boundaries.
- Measure ROI through time saved, questions resolved, and tickets reduced.

## Founder Decisions Already Embedded

1. Beachhead: HRMS / HR operations.
2. First product: AI HR Knowledge & Workflow Assistant.
3. Core category: Operational AI, not chatbot.
4. Primary buyer: HR leader, operations leader, founder/CEO, or IT-supported HR team.
5. Initial customer segment: SMB and mid-market organizations.
6. MVP must prove ROI within the first month.
7. Governance, permissions, audit logs, and source attribution are core MVP requirements.
