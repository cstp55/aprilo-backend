# Market and Financial Assumptions

## Purpose

This file records the working assumptions used across Chapters 3-12. These are not final facts. They are founder-modeling inputs that must be validated through customer discovery, pilots, partner interviews, and source upgrades.

## Market Sizing Logic

The Business Strategy Book should avoid using broad "AI market size" as TAM. The investor-grade model should size the market from the bottom up:

Potential target businesses x integration readiness x realistic annual contract value = addressable revenue opportunity.

## Gross Margin Assumptions

| Cost Area | Target |
|---|---:|
| Software gross margin after AI/cloud costs | 70%-85% long-term |
| Early gross margin | Lower acceptable during pilots if implementation learning is high |
| Professional services margin | 20%-50%, should not become the core business |
| AI cost as percentage of revenue | Target below 10%-20% after usage controls |

## Capital Strategy Assumptions

| Stage | Milestone Before Raise | Primary Use of Capital |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-seed | Working MVP, 3-5 design partners, evidence of urgent HR workflow pain | Build MVP, founder-led sales, first integrations |
| Seed | 20-50 paying customers, repeatable onboarding, early retention data | Team hiring, partner channel, security/compliance, additional platforms |
| Series A | $1M-$3M ARR, repeatable sales motion, strong retention, multi-platform expansion evidence | Scale GTM, enterprise readiness, marketplace/ecosystem |

## Assumptions To Validate First

1. HR buyers will pay for HR knowledge and workflow automation without needing the HRMS vendor to sell it.
2. SMB/mid-market companies will allow a third-party AI layer to connect to employee knowledge and HR systems if permissions and audit logs are strong.
3. Initial ACV can reach at least $3,600-$12,000 for HRMS customers after first ROI proof.
4. HR consultants and implementation partners can become a meaningful acquisition channel.
5. The platform architecture can reuse at least 50%-70% of core capabilities when expanding from HRMS to ecommerce and ERP.
