# Source Register

## Evidence Standard

Use this register to track evidence across the Business Strategy Book. A fact should not be used in the final investor version unless it has a source, date, scope, and confidence rating.

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Primary source, public company filing, official documentation, official survey/report, government or standards body |
| Medium | Reputable analyst/research firm public page, reputable media citing primary research, marketplace data provider |
| Low | Secondary estimate, blog, directory, or inferred estimate |

## Source Quality Notes

- Public AI adoption data is strong enough for directional market timing.
- Exact platform market share, installed-base, and SMB counts will require a later paid-data or direct-source validation pass.
- TAM/SAM/SOM should remain bottom-up and assumption-driven until customer discovery validates willingness to pay and integration readiness.
