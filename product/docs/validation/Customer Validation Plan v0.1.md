# Customer Validation Plan v0.1

## Objective

Validate whether SMB and mid-market companies will adopt and pay for an AI HR Knowledge & Workflow Assistant that reduces repetitive HR work and improves employee self-service.

## Validation Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Validation Method | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|
| HR teams repeatedly answer the same employee questions | Interviews with HR leaders and admins | 70%+ report recurring repetitive questions |
| HR documentation is scattered enough to create employee friction | Interviews and document workflow review | Customers describe multiple source locations |
| Customers trust AI if answers include sources and escalation | Concept testing | Buyers say citation/escalation reduces risk |
| HRMS is a good beachhead | Buyer interviews and pilot interest | 3-5 design partners agree to evaluate MVP |
| ROI can be measured in 30 days | Pilot metrics | Time saved, tickets deflected, or faster onboarding |
| Customers will pay | Pricing conversation and paid pilot offer | At least 3 prospects accept paid pilot or strong LOI |

## Target Interview Profiles

| Persona | Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| HR leaders | 10 | Buyer and pain owner |
| HR admins/generalists | 10 | Daily workflow owner |
| Founders/COOs | 5 | Budget holder in SMB |
| IT/security owners | 5 | Trust and implementation gatekeeper |
| HR consultants/implementation partners | 5 | Potential channel partners |

## Interview Questions

### HR Pain Discovery

1. What are the top five questions employees repeatedly ask HR?
2. How do employees ask these questions today?
3. How many HR questions or tickets does your team handle per week?
4. Which questions feel repetitive but still require careful answers?
5. Where does your HR knowledge live today?
6. How often are employees sent to the wrong document or outdated policy?
7. What happens when HR cannot answer quickly?

### Current Tools

1. What HRMS or HR tools do you use?
2. Do employees use Slack, Teams, email, portal, or helpdesk for HR questions?
3. Do you already use any AI tool for HR knowledge or support?
4. What works and what fails with current tools?
5. What would stop you from using AI in HR workflows?

### Trust and Security

1. What HR data should never be touched by an AI assistant?
2. Would source citations make answers more trustworthy?
3. Which topics must always escalate to a human?
4. What approval would IT/security require?
5. Are audit logs required?

### MVP Testing

1. If an assistant answered policy questions from approved HR documents, would your team use it?
2. Which first use case is most valuable: policy Q&A, onboarding support, or ticket deflection?
3. What would make the MVP a success after 30 days?
4. Who would need to approve a pilot?
5. Would you pay for a pilot if setup was low-risk?

### Pricing Discovery

1. How much time does HR spend on repeated questions each week?
2. What is the approximate cost of that time?
3. What monthly price would feel obviously worthwhile?
4. Would you prefer pricing by employee count, capability pack, or monthly usage?
5. Would you pay an implementation fee for setup?

## Pilot Design

### Pilot Duration

30-60 days.

### Pilot Scope

Start with:

- 20-100 employee users.
- Approved HR policy documents.
- HR knowledge Q&A.
- Escalation to HR admin.
- ROI reporting.

Avoid:

- Payroll.
- Medical/leave decisioning.
- Legal advice.
- Autonomous HRMS write actions.

### Pilot Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Resolution rate | 50%+ of questions resolved without HR escalation |
| Helpful rating | 70%+ helpful on rated answers |
| HR time saved | Clear positive estimate confirmed by HR |
| Knowledge gap value | HR identifies useful missing/weak content |
| Trust incidents | Zero high-severity access or privacy incidents |
| Conversion | Customer agrees to continue or expand |

## Design Partner Criteria

Good design partner:

- Has 50-1,000 employees.
- Has repeated HR questions.
- Has accessible HR documents.
- Has an HR owner willing to review answers.
- Accepts controlled MVP scope.
- Can give weekly feedback.
- Can become a case study if successful.

Poor design partner:

- Needs payroll automation first.
- Requires full enterprise compliance before any pilot.
- Has no organized policies or HR documents.
- Wants custom one-off workflows unrelated to platform strategy.
- Refuses to define success metrics.

## Evidence To Collect

1. Baseline weekly HR question volume.
2. Top repeated HR topics.
3. Current response time.
4. HR time spent answering repetitive questions.
5. Existing systems and knowledge sources.
6. Security objections.
7. Willingness to pay.
8. Pilot conversion intent.

## Validation Decision Gates

### Gate 1: Problem Validation

Proceed if at least 70% of target interviews confirm repeated HR questions are a meaningful pain.

### Gate 2: MVP Scope Validation

Proceed if at least 5 prospects agree that policy Q&A, onboarding support, or ticket deflection is valuable enough to pilot.

### Gate 3: Trust Validation

Proceed if customers accept the security model: approved knowledge, role access, citations, escalation, and audit logs.

### Gate 4: Pricing Validation

Proceed to paid pilots if at least 3 prospects accept a paid pilot or serious budget conversation.

### Gate 5: Expansion Validation

Expand beyond HRMS only after pilots show measurable value and repeatable setup.
